# Inspection plan and AS9102 FAI

## Ballooning
Every requirement on the drawing gets a balloon: each dimension, each GD&T control, each note, the material,
the finish and every specification called out. A "4X" feature is one balloon with four results, or four
sub-balloons (4.1-4.4), consistently. Record the balloon and drawing zone on the characteristic (`balloon`,
`drawing_zone`). Characteristic ids stay fixed; balloon numbers follow them.

## Inspection plan
For every characteristic, at least one `inspection_plan` row with stage (receiving, in-process, final, FAI),
method and gauge. KCs are normally checked in-process at the operation that makes them and again at FAI.
Gauge choice: resolution at least 1/10 of the tolerance; position at MMC by functional gauge or CMM; flatness
on a surface plate or CMM; material and finish by certificate.

## AS9102 forms (generated by `engsys build` into `<id>_FAI (Rev - X).xlsx`)
- **Form 1, part number accountability:** part number, name, serial, FAI report number, part and drawing rev,
  organization and supplier code, detail or assembly, full or partial FAI with the reason, sign-offs.
  For assemblies, list the sub-assembly and detail part numbers with their own FAI report numbers.
- **Form 2, product accountability:** raw material spec and certificate, special processes (heat treat,
  finish, NDT) with the processor, approval and certificate, and functional tests.
- **Form 3, characteristic accountability:** one line per balloon: characteristic number, reference
  location, designator (KC), requirement, actual result, designed tooling used, nonconformance number.

Fill `fai.results` in the record with every measured value (numbers for dimensions, "conforms"/"pass" for
attributes). Any value outside limits needs an NCR number, or G4 fails.

## When a new FAI is required (AS9102 Rev C, from memory: confirm against your copy)
Full FAI on a new part or new source. Partial (delta) FAI for a design change affecting fit, form or function,
a change in manufacturing source, process, inspection method, location or tooling, or a lapse in production
of two years. The `ecn` skill sets `fai_required` on each change.

Check with `engsys check <record> --gate G4 --report out/<NSN>/`.
